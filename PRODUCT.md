# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Casual and multi-TCG players playing **physical matches** at home, with friends, or at local game stores. They keep the phone **beside the table** — one hand free, distractions minimal, ambient light often dim (evening sessions, store lighting).

Their job: follow turn structure, remember what they already did, get honest nudges on common rules, and log results without turning the phone into a second game or a spreadsheet.

## Product Purpose

TurnWise is a **turn assistant for in-person TCG matches**. It guides phase flow, logs actions, applies simple repeatable validations, runs match timers, and saves history, stats, and achievements across sessions.

Success looks like: players finish matches with fewer forgotten rules, clearer turn flow, and a lightweight record of their play — while always understanding the app is a **tracker**, not a judge or board-state oracle.

## Brand Personality

**Focused · Trustworthy · Playful**

- **Focused** — Linear-like tool clarity: one primary task per screen, calm hierarchy, no visual noise at the table.
- **Trustworthy** — Honest about limits (tracker mode, manual confirmation, ℹ️ reminders); never pretends to see the physical board.
- **Playful** — Progress and delight through achievements, game-specific vocabulary, and tactile feedback — without tipping into toy-like or childish.

## Anti-references

- **Aggressive neon gaming UI** — loud gradients, cyberpunk chrome, overstimulating “gamer” aesthetics that fight focus at the table.
- **Childish / toy-like** — cartoon mascots, bubbly excess, infantilizing tone that undermines trust for mixed-age play groups.
- **Cluttered rulebook trackers** — dense walls of text, spreadsheet grids, or judge-console complexity on a phone screen.
- **Generic SaaS dashboards** — cream marketing surfaces, hero metrics, identical card grids (not the app shell TurnWise should feel like).

**Reference anchor:** [Linear](https://linear.app) — focused, minimal tool UI with confident restraint and crisp interaction hierarchy.

## Design Principles

1. **Honest tracker** — Surface what the app knows vs. what the player must confirm on the physical board. Never imply authority the product does not have.
2. **One task per glance** — During a match, the screen should answer one question at a time (current phase, next action, timer state) without competing focal points.
3. **Game-native language** — Vocabulary, flags, and coach tips follow each TCG’s terms (Exaurida, Descansado, Enjoo) so multi-game players feel understood, not translated.
4. **Playful progress, serious table** — Achievements, haptics, and micro-delight reward habit without sacrificing the calm needed mid-match.
5. **Recover gracefully** — Resume interrupted sessions, undo actions, and explain blocks with actionable copy; losing progress at the table erodes trust instantly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG 2.1 AA** as the baseline for contrast, touch targets, and readable copy.
- Respect **`prefers-reduced-motion`** for animations and haptic-adjacent visual pulses.
- Semantic colors (success / warning / danger / info) must remain distinguishable without relying on hue alone where status is critical.
- Portuguese (pt) is the primary UI language; copy stays plain and scannable for quick reads between turns.
